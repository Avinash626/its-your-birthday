A letter for you

Dear,

There are some dates that come and go quietly. And then there is 6th September.

Your birthday has always felt different to me. It feels like a festival I wait for. A day that makes me excited simply because it is your day. I don't know if words can ever properly explain what you mean to me, but today I want to try.

We've shared so many versions of ourselves over the years—the younger us, the growing-up us, the adventurous us, the silly us, the serious us, and all those little versions in between. When I look at these pictures, I don't just see photographs. I see pieces of our story.

I love the big moments, of course. But I think what I treasure most are the small ones: your smile, the way you look at me, the random conversations, the journeys, the food, the laughter, the quiet moments, and even the days when things aren't perfect.

On your birthday, my wish for you is simple: may you always have reasons to smile, courage for every difficult day, people who truly value you, and the freedom to become everything you want to be.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be beside you for many more birthdays—to watch you grow, celebrate you, annoy you a little, make you laugh, and create many more memories that we'll look back on someday.

Happy Birthday, my love. Thank you for being such a beautiful part of my life.

Always yours,
Avu ❤️
